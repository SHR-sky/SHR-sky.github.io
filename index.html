02 / About

Curiosity, made
measurable.

I am a researcher at the School of Electronic Information and Communications, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My interests sit at the intersection of wireless communication, sensing, statistical signal processing, and embedded systems.

I care about methods that work beyond controlled demonstrations: algorithms informed by physical structure, systems built on accessible hardware, and experiments that reveal how technology behaves in the world.
